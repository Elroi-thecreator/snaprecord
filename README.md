## 🛠️ The Extension Suite

### 1. SnapRecord — Simple Screen Recorder
A minimalist, distraction-free Chrome extension for 1-click screen, tab, and window capture with webcam facecam and instant seekable MP4/WebM export.
- **Best for**: Students, teachers, bug reports, and fast daily video messages.
- **Key Features**: 3 segmented capture modes, circular facecam PiP, floating HUD, and immediate preview player.

### 2. Recorder Studio Pro — Broadcast-Grade Screen & Meeting Studio
A full audio/video workstation inside Google Chrome for power users, developers, and podcasters.
- **Best for**: Google Meet conferences, webinars, and professional content creators.
- **Key Features**: Google Meet recording with **NO bot joining**, 48kHz dual audio mixing with noise gate, on-device AI silence auto-cut, multi-cut video trimmer studio, direct-to-disk zero-RAM recording, and separate WAV audio stem separation.

## 🔒 100% On-Device Privacy Guarantee
Both extensions operate under a strict **Zero-Cloud Privacy Protocol**:
- All audio processing, video compositing, and file exports execute 100% locally in your browser.
- No video or audio is ever uploaded to any cloud server.
- Compliant with HIPAA, FERPA, and corporate confidential data policies.
