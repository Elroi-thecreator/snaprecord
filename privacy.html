Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026 · Compliant with Chrome Web Store Policies
🔒 Zero-Cloud Guarantee: SnapRecord operates 100% locally on your computer. We do not track, collect, store, or transmit your recordings, camera feed, audio, keystrokes, or browsing data to any external server.

1. Overview

SnapRecord ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a lightweight screen and video recording extension for Google Chrome. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict privacy protections and details how the extension operates solely within the boundaries of your local browser environment.

2. Data Collection & Transmission

SnapRecord collects ZERO personal data.

  • No Cloud Storage: Audio and video recording chunks are processed in real-time in memory and stored temporarily in your local browser IndexedDB to prevent memory leaks.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: There are no analytics libraries, telemetry trackers, or third-party tracking pixels bundled inside the extension.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: No information is sold, shared, or transferred to third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

3. Permissions & Technical Usage

SnapRecord requests only the permissions strictly required to perform local screen, tab, and microphone recording upon explicit user command:

Permission Technical Purpose
tabCapture Captures high-definition audio and video from the currently active browser tab when the user selects the "Current Tab" mode.
activeTab Grants temporary authorization to interact with the active tab when the extension popup is opened.
storage Saves your recording preferences (e.g. format MP4/WebM, audio toggle state) locally on your device.
unlimitedStorage Allows temporary local IndexedDB buffering of video chunks during lengthy recording sessions to prevent browser crashes.
downloads Saves the finalized MP4 or WebM video file directly to your local computer's Downloads directory.

4. Camera & Microphone Access

Access to your microphone and webcam is requested solely for optional voice narration and circular picture-in-picture facecam overlay. Audio and video captured through the MediaDevices API remain strictly within your local session and are encoded directly into the downloaded output file.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Temporary chunks stored in IndexedDB during recording are automatically deleted once the download finishes or when a new recording starts. You can clear temporary cache at any time via your browser's site settings or by uninstalling the extension.
